# Retail Sales Analysis

A data analysis project using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to explore retail sales data and identify useful business insights.

## Project Overview

The goal of this project is to analyze retail sales data and understand business performance across different regions, product categories, customer segments, and shipping methods.

The analysis focuses on identifying sales patterns and areas where the business could explore further opportunities.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset

The project uses the Sample Superstore dataset, containing information about orders, customers, products, shipping methods, and sales.

**Note:** This version of the dataset does not include Profit, Quantity, or Discount columns. Therefore, the analysis focuses on sales rather than profitability or units sold.

## Analysis Performed

- Sales by Region
- Sales by Category and Sub-Category
- Customer Segment Analysis
- Sales Trends Over Time
- Shipping Mode Analysis
- Top Customers by Sales
- Sales by State and City

## Key Findings

- The West region generated the highest total sales, contributing approximately 31.4%.
- Technology was the highest-performing product category, contributing approximately 36.6% of sales.
- Phones and Chairs were the leading sub-categories by sales.
- Consumer customers generated the highest total sales.
- Sales increased from approximately $479,856 in 2015 to $722,052 in 2018.
- Standard Class was the most commonly used shipping method.
- The top 10 customers contributed approximately 6.8% of total sales.
- California and New York together contributed approximately 33.3% of total sales.
- The top 10 cities contributed approximately 44.5% of total sales.

## Business Recommendations

- Continue building on the strong sales performance of the West and East regions.
- Give attention to high-sales product sub-categories.
- Explore opportunities to increase sales among Corporate and Home Office customers.
- Investigate the factors contributing to strong sales in California and New York.
- Monitor sales trends and changes in yearly growth.
- Monitor shipping performance and customer feedback.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Python and data analysis techniques can be used to explore retail sales data, identify patterns, and generate business insights.

It was completed as a hands-on project to develop practical data analysis skills.
